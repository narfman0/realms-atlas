fetches): year pages (-3859, 1, 261, 1359, 1360, 1489, 1490), Days of Thunder, First Flowering, Crown Wars,
Age of Humanity, Present Age, Era of Upheaval, Netheril, Cormanthyr, Cormyr, Myth Drannor, Weeping War,

## Disputes

| Event | Conflict | Chosen |
|---|---|---|
| Companion over Elturel | Wiki *Elturel* page gives 1444 DR (citing SCAG p.80); wiki *The Companion* page gives 1442 DR for the same vampire episode, citing the same page. Elturel's page also says "after the 1430s". | **1444**, per the brief and the Elturel article. |
| Tyranny of Dragons | No year in the adventures. Greg Marks (forum post) said 1489 DR, but the novel *Archmage* (set 1485–1486) refers to events after *Rise of Tiamat*. The wiki uses "1480s". | **1489** (designer statement, and the brief). Could reasonably be moved to ~1485. |
| Tomb of Annihilation / death curse | No canon year; the wiki brackets it between 1488 DR (Port Nyanzaru's independence) and 1492 DR (Volo's book tour ending before *Dragon Heist*). | **1490**, as the brief asks; it falls inside the range. |
| Descent into Avernus | Often dated 1494 DR in fan timelines. The wiki's Elturel and Companion articles put the city's fall in **1492 DR**, matching SPEC. | **1492**. |
| Baldur's Gate 3 | In-game evidence points to either Eleasis 21 or after Uktar 3, 1492 DR; one scene suggests 1498. The wiki uses 1492. | **1492**. |
| Era of Upheaval end | The wiki says Ao ended it in 1489 DR, or treats it as running to 1488 (*Present Age* article). SPEC uses the era for 1358–1385 only. | Era band kept at **1358–1385** per SPEC; Spellplague (1385–1482) and Second Sundering (1482–1487) get their own bands. The 1489 decree is mentioned in the Present Age blurb. |
| Second Sundering end | The wiki says the Sundering ended "as 1487 DR came to a close", but some summaries run it to 1488 or 1489. | **1482–1487** per SPEC. |
| Founding of Cormyr | The 1 DR year page says "Cormyr is founded"; the Cormyr article and the traditional date put it at 26 DR (Faerlthann Obarskyr crowned). | **26 DR**. |
| "Founding of Cormanthyr (261)" | The brief's 261 DR is really the **Opening of Myth Drannor** (mythal completed, city renamed). The realm of Cormanthyr itself dates to about **-4000 DR** (first Coronal, Kahvoerm Irithyl). | Both appear: *Cormanthyr founded* at -4000 and *Opening of Myth Drannor* at 261. |
| Weeping War | The war runs 711–714 DR; the final siege of Myth Drannor began 21 Kythorn 714. | **714** (fall of the city), per SPEC. |
| Zhentil Keep founding | 747 DR is when Orlephar Flostren began fortifying the site. Older sources describe earlier settlement there. | **747**. |
| Zhentil Keep destruction | The city was mostly ruined in 1368 (with the dragon and giant assault in 1369) and destroyed by the shadovar in 1383. | Both are listed as minor events. |
| Tilverton | Some summaries put it in 1369 (Year of the Gauntlet); the wiki is clear on **1372** (Mirtul 27, shadovar attack). | **1372**, per SPEC. |
| Gauntlgrym reclaimed | The brief says "1480s". The wiki: drow held the forge by 1484; Bruenor's army retook the city in **1486**. | **1486**. |
| Tuigan Horde | The invasion spans 1359–1360. The schema has only one `year` per event. | **1359**; the summary covers 1360 (Azoun's victory). |
| Rage of Demons | Demons are summoned in 1485–1486; Demogorgon arrives in 1486. | **1485**; the summary notes 1486. |
| Era boundaries before -3900 | The wiki places the Days of Thunder near -35000 to -30000, the Time of Dragons up to -24000, the First Flowering -24000 to -12000, and the Crown Wars -12000 to -9000. To stay within 10 eras, and because the scrubber starts near -3900, these are merged into one band, **Days of Thunder & First Flowering (-35000 to -3859)**. | Merged. |

## Conventions
- Era `to` equals the next era's `from` (the boundary year belongs to the later era).
- Multi-year events use their starting year; the summary mentions the span.
- `placeIds` use only SPEC roster ids. Off-roster places (Greenest, Well of Dragons, Thesk, Tymanther, Avernus) appear only in summary text.
